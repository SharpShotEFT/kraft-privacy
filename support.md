# Kraft support

[Privacy](./) · [Support](./support.html) · [Terms](./terms.html)

**Effective date:** 5 September 2026

Contact **support@kraftlift.com** with your app version, device/OS, reproduction steps and non-sensitive error. Never send passwords, recovery codes, sign-in tokens or raw purchase tokens. Crop unrelated private information from screenshots.

The support mailbox is monitored through Gmail. Resolved support correspondence is retained for 12 months after resolution. Unresolved authorization/revocation/deletion cases are reviewed weekly; this is not a guaranteed response or provider-resolution deadline.

Guidance applies where the installed version offers the described feature. Kraft's iOS 1.3 release is being prepared; the iPhone-specific instructions do not announce its availability. Current Android versions may offer different sign-in options.

## Accounts

Already using Kraft on Android? Use that same Google account on iPhone. To add another sign-in method, sign into the existing Kraft account and select Connect Apple/Google in Account.

Hide My Email may create a different account. Matching emails manually does not merge accounts or transfer purchases. If credentials belong to another Kraft account, contact support; do not delete an account as a workaround. Linking does not automatically enable sync.

For interrupted/revoked Apple sign-in, follow the reconnect/clear-unfinished-sign-in instructions and authorize afresh. Local workouts remain available. Do not repeat a purchase to fix sign-in trouble.

## Missing Basic/Pro

Sign into the original Kraft account and use Restore purchases while online. If an old Google purchase requests Android restoration, open Kraft on Android with that Kraft account and purchasing Google Play account, restore once, then retry on iPhone.

“Unverified” means access cannot currently be confirmed, not that you should pay again. Previously verified offline access is bounded by proof/entitlement expiry. A new platform's empty store history alone does not erase the other platform's purchase.

Refunded/revoked Basic and expired Pro do not grant current access. Pending/Ask to Buy may require store approval. Purchases do not automatically move between Kraft accounts; automatic Apple rebinding after deleting/recreating an account is unsupported. Contact support before irreversible account changes.

## Cancel Pro

Account → Manage Pro subscription follows the store that bills you, even on the other platform.

- Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Kraft → Cancel Subscription. [Apple instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428).
- Google Play: use subscriptions management for the purchasing Google account. [Google instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481).

Deleting Kraft/account, signing out or disabling sync does not cancel billing. Refund outcomes depend on the billing store and applicable consumer rights; support cannot promise one.

## Backup and recovery

Each online backup is a deliberately confirmed, device-encrypted upload for the displayed account. A successful upload replaces its previous backup; it is not automatic backup. Sync is separate and server-readable, with encryption in transit and at rest. Decline to keep the snapshot local. Disable sync to stop future transfers from that device, not to delete existing cloud records. Signing out requires fresh sync enablement after signing in.

Keep the recovery code privately. A new device may need it; support cannot reconstruct it. Ordinary sign-out/provider revocation retains account-scoped recovery material. Uninstall/reinstall is not guaranteed key recovery or secure erasure.

Kraft verifies an existing backup's key before replacing it. A wrong code must not overwrite the backup. Reading an existing backup remains possible after Pro lapses for an active, admitted account with its key; creating/replacing one needs Pro.

## Delete data

Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account, cloud training/body data and backups, subject to any reported queued storage cleanup. Limited purchase/security records and unfinished provider-revocation records may remain for the purposes described in the privacy policy. Email support@kraftlift.com to request deletion without the app or discuss cloud-data deletion; ownership verification may be necessary. Queued storage cleanup and Apple revocation have their own completion states; contact support if a warning persists.

Local workouts remain separate. Settings → Clear all data removes local records. External exports/recovery codes remain under your control. Cancel Pro separately to stop renewal.
