Manage through the billing store: [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428) or [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481). Do not delete/recreate an account to recover a purchase. Automatic Apple rebinding to a new Kraft account is unsupported; contact support first.
